# UART decode of "Hiмир!" (ASCII + UTF-8, no separator) at 115200 8N1,
# framed on the boundary: 48/H and 69/i with their ASCII labels, then the
# two-byte UTF-8 sequence D0 BC = U+043C. Bit grid on the trace.
#
# The record edge cuts one frame of the endless stream; the decoder counts
# it as a framing error, which is what it is.
#
# AUTO, slow the timebase until the record holds a whole message, STOP,
# SHIFT+EDGE to decode, TRIG_UP x4 to the boundary, SHIFT+LEFT to 20 us/div.
# The device boots into the oscilloscope by itself (Startup = Oscilloscope,
# the default), so there is no launcher row left to press MODE on here
